-   **Key takeaway:** Fiori/UI5 → OData → ABAP/S/4HANA.

## Unit 4 --- Content Management

## Unit 6 --- Content Administration

[Open Unit

## Unit 7 --- Adaptation

[Open Unit
7](https://learning.sap.com/courses/learning-the-basics-of-sap-fiori)
